Belief, Knowledge & Life

The hardest part was not having questions. It was not knowing where they belonged.

Some began with science and ended in philosophy. Some began with Deen and became questions about people, interpretation and character. Others appeared quietly in ordinary life, in what we call success and what we gradually learn to value.

I am a student inside these questions, not someone writing from the other side of them.

This journey follows what happens when the questions are allowed to stay open long enough to be examined properly. I will share what I read, what holds up, what unsettles an earlier assumption, and the moments when the question itself has to change.
