Opening · Part 7

From notes recorded in September and October 2026

Recording the learning while it happens

  • Belief and conduct
  • Science and interpretation
  • Philosophy and everyday life

The subjects I need may include psychology, philosophy, sociology, genetics, Qur’an and Arabic. I do not want a list of disciplines to become another plan that takes the place of learning. I want the question to lead me to the work it needs. A source should help me test or develop the thought, including showing that I have misunderstood, rather than replace it with a ready-made answer.

I want to record this while it is happening. The confusion matters, but so do the excitement of learning and the moments when a question becomes clearer. If I share only conclusions, I lose the part I most want to preserve: how I came to ask, what I thought the answer might be, and what made me reconsider it.

Perhaps one person will need this someday. Maybe someone will feel this same difficulty and find a question here that they recognise. I also want serious academic work to emerge where there is something worth developing, something another person could find and use in a literature review. But I do not know yet what that work will be. I want to record the problems as well as the conclusions.
