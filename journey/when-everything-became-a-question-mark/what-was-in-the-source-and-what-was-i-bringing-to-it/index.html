When Everything Became a Question Mark · Chapter One · Entry 07

What Was in the Source, and What Was I Bringing to It?

11 October 2026·English

Once I started reading philosophy, something slightly annoying happened.

The psychology lecture was no longer the only thing I was taking apart.

I would be reading about evidence, inference, assumptions, how we know something, and then suddenly remember a completely different conversation from my Deen studies.

A verse would be discussed.

Then its meaning.

Then how it should be applied.

And somewhere along the way, the source and the explanation around it could begin to feel like one thing.

That sounded familiar.

In psychology, I had started with evolution, the brain, biological mechanisms.

Then came conclusions about the mind.

About God.

Different subject. Different sources. Different methods.

But I kept circling back to one question.

Where did the source end, and the person explaining it begin?

I had first noticed the problem while trying to understand a psychology lecture.

Now it was following me back into Deen.

And another part of the question was becoming harder to ignore.

What was actually in the source, what had been added around it, and what was I bringing into it myself?
