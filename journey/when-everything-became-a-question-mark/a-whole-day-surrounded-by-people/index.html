When Everything Became a Question Mark · Chapter One · Entry 05

A Whole Day Surrounded by People

11 October 2026·English

The strange thing is, it was actually a good day.

We had gone camping with friends, something I do not normally do. People had organised everything with so much care. There was food, children playing, people moving around, talking, deciding what to cook next.

I enjoyed being there.

And I was almost never alone.

For most of the day, I was sitting with the same group of people. The conversations moved easily from one thing to another: children, family, everyday problems, what someone had said, what someone had done, what needed cooking.

Everyone seemed to have something to add.

I mostly listened.

Not because anything was wrong with the conversation.

I just did not have much to say.

The things occupying my mind at that point felt very far away from everything being discussed around me.

For days, I had been thinking about the lecture. About the brain and the mind. About evolution. About God. About what science could actually establish. At the same time, I was still carrying everything that had happened in my work with people teaching Deen.

Those questions were taking up a surprising amount of space in my head.

At some point, I tried to bring a little of that into the conversation.

And I noticed something.

People would listen.

But after a while, it stopped feeling like a conversation.

I would be explaining something, trying to show why I found it interesting or troubling, and the other person would become quiet. Sometimes I could almost feel their attention drifting.

It was not disagreement.

That would actually have been easier.

There was simply nowhere for the thought to go.

So I would stop.

And the conversation would return to something else.

Then a few hours later it would happen again.

Someone would talk about family, children, food, plans, some ordinary thing that had happened.

I would listen.

I liked the people around me.

I was grateful to be there.

I was having a good time.

And still, as the hours passed, I became more and more aware of how quiet I had become.

I spent almost the whole day surrounded by people.

What I could not seem to find was somewhere to put the questions filling my head.
