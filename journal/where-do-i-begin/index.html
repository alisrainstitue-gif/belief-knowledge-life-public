Where Do I Begin?

29 September 2026·100 words·English

I thought learning would help me feel more settled. Lately, it has left me with more questions.

I have seen religious knowledge sit alongside behaviour I struggle to understand. While studying science, I sometimes wonder how much of an explanation comes from evidence, and how much comes from the person explaining it.

So where does that leave someone like you and me?

I am beginning a journey through these questions: what shapes our thinking, whom we trust, and how closely we live what we believe.

I will share what I learn, what unsettles me, and what gradually changes my mind.
