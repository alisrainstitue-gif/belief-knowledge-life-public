About BKL and the Author

About this journey

This began with questions I could no longer keep in separate parts of my life. What does knowledge change in us? How do we decide whom to trust? What belongs to a source, what comes from its interpretation, and what do we bring to it ourselves?

I want to understand more carefully. That means giving sources my own time and attention, examining the reasoning around them, and noticing where my expectations may shape what I see. Teachers and other writers remain part of that learning. So does the work of understanding for myself.

I am sharing the journey while it is still unfolding. Some entries follow an experience; others stay with a passage, an argument or something I have discovered. A question may become clearer, an understanding may change, or a conclusion may become strong enough to explain and defend. I do not know in advance where every inquiry will lead.

About the author

My background is in science, and research remains one of the things I love: working with evidence, recognising patterns and finding connections that were not obvious before.

Alongside that, I have been studying Deen and reading the Qur’an. Questions about what we know have gradually become questions about how we live, what we trust and what we consider worthwhile.

This journal follows those questions across the different areas of learning they lead me into. I write as a student, with the experience I already bring and much that I still need to understand.

Reading together

What I choose to share will influence your reading too. I hope that showing the steps, sources and assumptions makes that influence easier to examine. You are welcome to disagree, question my reasoning and follow an inquiry of your own.
