Chapter Two

A note before reading

These pages are a student’s journal of reading the Qur’an: the questions, confusion, excitement and changing understanding that come with learning. I am trying sincerely, with the capacity Allah Subhanahu wa Taala has given me. I share the resources I encounter and the steps through which I reach an understanding, including what remains unresolved.

These entries are not religious rulings or final conclusions; they record a student’s developing understanding. Please read them as part of that continuing inquiry, with room to question the reasoning and examine the sources for yourself.
